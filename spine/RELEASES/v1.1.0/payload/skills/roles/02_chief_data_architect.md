# Chief Data Architect — Full Skill Set

**Track:** Bridge between IC and Executive
**Mandate:** Own the master architectural vision for all data assets across the organization. Translate business strategy into a single coherent data architecture standard.

> Prerequisite: full Enterprise Data Architect skill set. This role adds strategy, governance authority, and cross-org control on top.

---

## 1. Architectural Vision & Strategy

- Enterprise-wide data architecture blueprint ownership
- Multi-year data architecture roadmap
- Target-state vs current-state gap analysis
- Build-vs-buy-vs-partner decisioning
- Technology lifecycle and deprecation strategy
- Aligning data architecture to business strategy and revenue model

## 2. Standards & Governance Authority

- Authoring binding architectural standards
- Architecture Review Board ownership / chairing
- Enforcement mechanisms (gates, sign-off, exception process)
- Naming, modeling, and contract conventions org-wide
- Reference architecture libraries
- Architecture Decision Record (ADR) governance
- Technical debt registry and remediation prioritization

## 3. Enterprise Data Management

- Master Data Management strategy (not just implementation)
- Enterprise data catalog and lineage ownership
- Data domain ownership model (who owns what)
- Data product strategy (data-as-product)
- Single source of truth definition and enforcement
- Reference and taxonomy governance

## 4. Data Mesh / Federated Architecture

- Domain-oriented decentralized ownership design
- Federated computational governance
- Self-serve data platform strategy
- Cross-domain interoperability standards
- Balancing autonomy vs central control

## 5. Platform & Ecosystem Strategy

- Multi-cloud / hybrid-cloud architecture decisions
- Vendor and platform portfolio strategy
- Lock-in risk assessment and mitigation
- Integration architecture across heterogeneous systems
- Migration and modernization strategy (legacy → target)

## 6. Governance, Risk & Compliance (Strategic)

- Enterprise data governance framework design
- Regulatory architecture (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI, regional law)
- Data residency and sovereignty architecture
- Privacy-by-design and security-by-design enforcement
- Risk modeling for data assets
- Audit-readiness as an architectural property

## 7. Financial & Commercial

- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling for data estate
- FinOps strategy for data platforms
- Cost allocation and chargeback models
- ROI modeling for architecture investments
- Budget ownership for data architecture initiatives

## 8. Cross-Functional Leadership

- Stakeholder management (engineering, product, security, finance, legal)
- Executive communication and architecture storytelling
- Influence without direct authority
- Conflict resolution between competing domain needs
- Vendor negotiation (technical scope)
- Roadmap negotiation with product and business

## 9. Organizational Design

- Data team topology design (platform, enablement, domain teams)
- Defining architect career ladders and competencies
- Center of Excellence (CoE) establishment
- Operating model for data (centralized vs federated vs hybrid)
- Mentoring and growing senior/principal architects

## 10. Emerging Technology Evaluation

- AI/ML data infrastructure (feature stores, vector DBs, RAG pipelines)
- Real-time analytics and streaming-first architectures
- Lakehouse evolution and open table formats
- Continuous evaluation framework for new tech
- Proof-of-concept governance

## 11. Business Alignment

- Translating P&L drivers into data capabilities
- Linking architecture to revenue, conversion, and control
- Data monetization architecture (internal and external products)
- Business capability mapping to data systems
- Outcome-based architecture (architecture justified by business result)

## 12. Deep Technical Retention

- Maintain hands-on credibility across modeling, pipelines, streaming, cloud
- Ability to review and reject any team's design on technical merit
- System-level performance and scale judgment
- Distributed systems theory (CAP, consistency models, consensus)

---

## Learning Sequence

1. Complete Enterprise Data Architect mastery (prerequisite)
2. Standards authoring + architecture governance authority
3. Enterprise data management + MDM strategy
4. Data Mesh / federated governance design
5. Platform portfolio + multi-cloud strategy
6. Strategic GRC + financial/TCO modeling
7. Cross-functional leadership + influence
8. Organizational design + operating model
9. Business alignment + data monetization architecture
