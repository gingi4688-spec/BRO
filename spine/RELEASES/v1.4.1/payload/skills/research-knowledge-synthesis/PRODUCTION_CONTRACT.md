## The 10 answers / 10 պատասխանը

1. **Artifacts** — research briefs, evidence tables, claim-verification/fact-check sheets, source-triage (CRAAP), systematic comparisons of conflicting sources, calibrated-confidence write-ups, literature reviews. / research brief, evidence table, fact-check sheet, source triage, comparison, lit review։
2. **Inputs** — the question/claim · the sources available · the confidence bar · scope/recency needs. / հարց/claim · sources · confidence bar · scope/recency։
3. **Evidence** — every load-bearing claim maps to a real, correctly-attributed source *actually seen*, or is labeled unverified · volatile facts dated + verify-before-use · conflicts surfaced not smoothed. / claim→real source · labeled unverified · dated volatile · conflicts surfaced։
4. **Verification gate** — source exists + says what's cited (no misattribution) · confidence calibrated · volatile facts flagged · primary-vs-secondary distinguished. / source real+says-it · calibrated · flagged · primary/secondary։
5. **GREEN** — every load-bearing claim carries a real, correctly-attributed, seen source (or an honest "unverified" label), confidence is calibrated, conflicts are shown — reviewed independently (L18). / real cited sources or labeled, calibrated, conflicts shown, independent։
6. **YELLOW** — synthesis produced but a claim's source unverified (honestly labeled), or confidence not calibrated. / source unverified (labeled) կամ confidence չcalibrated։
7. **RED** — a fabricated source (made-up paper/DOI/quote/stat) · a misattributed source (real source cited for a claim it doesn't make) · a volatile fact asserted from memory as current. / fabricated source · misattribution · volatile-from-memory։
8. **Approval-required (D0)** — none of its own; publishing/acting on a synthesis externally is Gev's. / սեփականը չկա. external publish՝ Gev-ի։
9. **Refusal / boundary** — #1 gate: never fabricate or misattribute a source; no source seen → no citation (label unverified); manufacture no statistic (route raw-data modeling to data-science). / #1 gate՝ ոչ fabrication/misattribution, ոչ manufactured statistic։
10. **Eval cases (scaffold, DECLARED)** — `tests/eval-prompts.md` (synthesis/fact-check/triage prompts) + `tests/red-team-prompts.md` (fabricate-a-source + misattribution + memory-volatile-fact traps).
