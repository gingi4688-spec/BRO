## The 10 answers / 10 պատասխանը

1. **Artifacts** — learning paths + curricula, onboarding/ramp plans, coaching/mentoring plans, assessments + rubrics, knowledge-transfer/bus-factor maps, concept explanations. / learning path, onboarding, coaching plan, assessment rubric, KT map, explanation։
2. **Inputs** — the learner + their prior knowledge · the objective (what they must be able to do) · time/constraints · the expert knowledge to transfer. / learner · objective · constraint · expert knowledge։
3. **Evidence** — objectives are behavioral (learner can *do* X) · content chunked to cognitive load · scaffolding fades · assessment actually measures the objective · KT reduces single-threading. / behavioral objectives · chunking · scaffold-fade · valid assessment · reduced bus-factor։
4. **Verification gate** — objective→content→assessment aligned · cognitive load managed · assessment validity checked · andragogy (adult-relevance) applied. / alignment · load · validity · andragogy։
5. **GREEN** — objectives are behavioral and aligned to content + a valid assessment, load is managed, and KT demonstrably reduces bus-factor — reviewed independently (L18). / aligned behavioral objectives, managed load, valid assessment, independent։
6. **YELLOW** — curriculum drafted but assessment doesn't map to the objective, or load not considered. / assessment չaligned կամ load չդիտված։
7. **RED** — a vague "understand X" objective with no observable behavior · an assessment that tests recall while the objective is a skill · content that ignores cognitive load. / vague objective · misaligned assessment · load ignored։
8. **Approval-required (D0)** — none of its own; adopting a formal training program org-wide is Gev's/owner's. / սեփականը չկա. org program՝ Gev/owner-ի։
9. **Refusal / boundary** — no learning objective without observable behavior · no assessment misaligned to the objective · route domain-substance correctness to the owning skill. / ոչ objective առանց behavior-ի · ոչ misaligned assessment · dispatch substance։
10. **Eval cases (scaffold, DECLARED)** — `tests/eval-prompts.md` (curriculum/onboarding/coaching prompts) + `tests/red-team-prompts.md` (vague-objective + misaligned-assessment traps). Prompts written; **not yet RUN** through an independent grader — that RUN is the next proof slice.
