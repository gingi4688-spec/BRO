## 0. Ո՞Ր ՈՍՊՆՅԱԿԸ ԵՐԲ (the three lenses)

Revenue is one number seen through three jobs. Most mistakes = optimizing one lens while another bleeds (e.g. crushing acquisition CAC while retention rots — you're filling a bucket faster than it drains and calling it growth).

| Lens | Horizon | Ի՞նչ ա որոշում | Question it asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demand & Marketing** | top-of-funnel / awareness→interest | *Որտեղ* ա ապրում քո buyer-ը, ո՞ր channel-ն ա հասնում իրեն, ի՞նչ message-ով | "Are the right people hearing the right promise, at a cost we can pay back?" |
| **Sales** | mid-funnel / interest→closed-won | *Ո՞նց* ա մի human (կամ product) տանում ընտրությունից մինչև signed contract | "Is this deal qualified, multi-threaded, and moving — or am I being slow-no'd?" |
| **Growth & Retention** | full-loop / activate→retain→expand→refer | *Ինչ ա* ստիպում system-ին compound-վել՝ ամ cohort-ը սնում ա հաջորդը | "Does each cohort make the next cohort cheaper to acquire and worth more?" |

**Rule of use:** start from the **growth/retention** question (does the engine compound, does the value land), descend to the **sales** question (is this specific deal real and movable), and only then turn on the **demand** tap. Pouring demand into a funnel that doesn't activate or retain is the single most common, most expensive error in this domain. Volume last, not first.

**HY (կանոնը):** սկսիր **growth/retention**-ի հարցից (engine-ը compound-վո՞ւմ ա, value-ն հասնո՞ւմ ա), իջի՛ր **sales**-ի հարցին (այս կոնկրետ deal-ը իրակա՞ն ա ու շարժվո՞ւմ ա), ու միայն հետո բացիր **demand**-ի ծորակը։ Demand լցնել funnel-ի մեջ, որ չի activate/retain անում, այս ոլորտի ամենահաճախ ու ամենաթանկ սխալն ա։ Volume-ը վերջում, ոչ թե սկզբում։

## 1. Principles (govern everything below)

1. **Conversion beats volume.** More leads into a leaky funnel just wastes more leads. Fix the rate before pouring in traffic. A funnel is a *multiplicative* chain — a 2× improvement at the worst-converting step beats a 2× traffic increase, and costs nothing in CAC.
   *HY:* Conversion-ը գերակա ա volume-ից։ Ավելի շատ lead ծակ funnel-ի մեջ պարզապես ավելի շատ lead ա վատնում։ Ուղղի՛ր rate-ը traffic լցնելուց առաջ։ Funnel-ը *բազմապատկվող* շղթա ա — ամենավատ converting step-ի 2× բարելավումը գերակա ա 2× traffic-ից ու CAC չի արժենում։
2. **Sell the outcome, not the feature.** People buy a better version of themselves / their business, not specs. Features are *evidence* the outcome is real; lead with the outcome, support with the feature. (JTBD: "people don't want a quarter-inch drill, they want a quarter-inch hole" — and really they want the shelf up and the spouse happy.)
   *HY:* Վաճառիր outcome-ը, ոչ թե feature-ը։ Մարդիկ գնում են իրենց/իրենց բիզնեսի ավելի լավ տարբերակը, ոչ թե spec-եր։ Feature-ները *ապացույց* են, որ outcome-ն իրական ա. առաջ տար outcome-ը, հենի feature-ով։
3. **Qualify hard, early.** Time is the scarce resource. Disqualify bad fits fast; concentrate on winnable, valuable deals. A fast "no" is a gift; a slow "maybe" is the most expensive thing in a pipeline.
   *HY:* Qualify արա կոշտ ու վաղ։ Ժամանակն ա սակավ ռեսուրսը։ Վատ fit-երը արագ disqualify արա. կենտրոնացիր շահելի, արժեքավոր deal-երի վրա։ Արագ «ոչ»-ը նվեր ա. դանդաղ «միգուցե»-ն pipeline-ի ամենաթանկ բանն ա։
4. **Speed of cash > size of pipeline.** A smaller deal closed this week often beats a bigger one "maybe next quarter." Cash compounds, optionality compounds, and a closed deal is a reference and an expansion seed; an open deal is a hope.
   *HY:* Cash-ի արագությունը > pipeline-ի չափից։ Այս շաբաթ փակած ավելի փոքր deal-ը հաճախ գերակա ա «միգուցե հաջորդ եռամսյակ»-ի ավելի մեծից։ Cash-ը compound-վում ա, փակած deal-ը reference ու expansion-ի սերմ ա. բաց deal-ը՝ հույս։
5. **Retention is the real growth engine.** Acquisition fills a bucket; retention determines whether it holds water. **Net revenue retention (NRR)** is the truest single number in recurring revenue — above 100% means you grow even with zero new logos. Optimize the bucket before the tap.
   *HY:* Retention-ն ա իրական growth engine-ը։ Acquisition-ը լցնում ա դույլը. retention-ը որոշում ա՝ ջուր պահո՞ւմ ա։ **NRR**-ն ա recurring revenue-ի ամենաճշմարիտ թիվը — 100%-ից վեր նշանակում ա՝ աճում ես առանց նոր logo-ի։
6. **The funnel is a system, not a sequence.** Stages interact: a wrong-promise ad pollutes activation; a heavy sales-assist masks a broken product; a price too low caps the channels you can afford. Diagnose end-to-end before fixing one step.
   *HY:* Funnel-ը system ա, ոչ թե հաջորդականություն։ Stage-երը փոխազդում են. սխալ-խոստումով գովազդը աղտոտում ա activation-ը. ծանր sales-assist-ը թաքցնում ա կոտրված product-ը. շատ ցածր գինը կտրում ա channel-ները, որ կարող ես թույլ տալ։
7. **Match the motion to the deal economics.** ACV (annual contract value) dictates the legal motion: tiny ACV → self-serve/PLG (a human can't touch it profitably); large ACV → high-touch enterprise sales (you can't NOT touch it). Mismatch = either burning salaries on $20 deals or leaving $200k deals to a signup form. (The "$1 to $1,000,000 ACV" ladder — read it before designing any motion.)
   *HY:* Համապատասխանեցրու motion-ը deal-ի economics-ին։ ACV-ն թելադրում ա motion-ը. փոքր ACV → self-serve/PLG (human-ը շահութաբեր չի կարող դիպչել). մեծ ACV → high-touch enterprise sales (չես կարող ՉԴիպչել)։ Անհամապատասխանություն = կամ աշխատավարձ ես այրում $20 deal-երի վրա, կամ $200k deal-երը թողնում ես signup form-ին։
8. **Price is the strongest profit lever you have, and the least used.** A 1% price improvement, fully realized, typically moves operating profit more than a 1% gain in volume or cost — yet teams reflexively reach for discounting and traffic. Earn the right to charge, then charge.
   *HY:* Գինն ա ամենաուժեղ profit lever-ը ու ամենաքիչ օգտագործվածը։ 1% գնի բարելավումը, լիարժեք իրացված, սովորաբար operating profit-ն ավելի շատ ա շարժում, քան 1% volume կամ cost-ի շահույթ — բայց team-երը ռեֆլեքսով discount ու traffic են ընտրում։
9. **Trust is the conversion substrate.** Every step is a small trust transaction. Risk-reversal (guarantees, trials, references, proof) converts better than persuasion, because it moves the buyer's perceived downside, not just their perceived upside.
   *HY:* Վստահությունն ա conversion-ի հիմքը։ Ամեն քայլ փոքր trust-գործարք ա։ Risk-reversal-ը (երաշխիք, trial, reference, ապացույց) ավելի լավ ա convert անում, քան համոզումը, որովհետև շարժում ա գնորդի ընկալվող *վնասը*, ոչ միայն ընկալվող օգուտը։
10. **Don't confuse the model with the motion with the metric.** The *business model* (recurring/transactional/marketplace — Skill 05) decides what you can charge; the *go-to-market motion* (who and what does the selling) decides how the value is delivered to a buyer; the *metrics* (§2) tell you whether either is working. A right motion on the wrong model still bleeds; a great metric dashboard on a broken funnel just measures the bleeding. Keep the three distinct and aligned.
    *HY:* Մի՛ շփոթիր model-ը motion-ի ու metric-ի հետ։ *Business model*-ը (recurring/transactional/marketplace — Skill 05) որոշում ա, թե ինչ կարող ես գանձել. *go-to-market motion*-ը՝ ով ու ինչն ա վաճառում. *metric*-ները՝ թե երկուսից որն ա աշխատում։ Ճիշտ motion սխալ model-ի վրա դեռ արյունահոսում ա. գեղեցիկ dashboard կոտրված funnel-ի վրա ուղղակի արյունահոսությունն ա չափում։

## 2. THE FUNNEL & THE METRICS — the diagnostic core

### 2.1 The funnel vocabulary (AIDA → TOFU/MOFU/BOFU → AARRR)
Three lenses on the same journey; use them at different altitudes.

- **AIDA (Lewis, 1898 — the oldest funnel)** — **A**ttention → **I**nterest → **D**esire → **A**ction. The *psychological* arc of a single buyer; useful for crafting a message, ad, or page (cross-link **Skill 08** for the copy itself). A page that wins attention but never builds desire converts the curious, not the committed.
- **TOFU / MOFU / BOFU (the marketing stage map)** — **Top** of funnel (awareness — broad, educational, cheap-per-touch), **Middle** (consideration — comparison, proof, nurture), **Bottom** (decision — demo, trial, pricing, the close). Map every asset and channel to the stage it serves; a BOFU asset (case study, ROI calculator) at the top wastes itself, a TOFU asset (broad blog) at the bottom doesn't close.
- **AARRR (pirate metrics — McClure)** — **Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral.** The *full-lifecycle, post-acquisition* map and the one Bro instruments by default, because it includes what AIDA/TOFU stop short of: whether the customer stayed, paid more, and brought others.

**HY:** Նույն ճանապարհի երեք ոսպնյակ՝ տարբեր բարձրությունից։ **AIDA**-ն (Lewis, 1898)՝ մեկ գնորդի հոգեբանական աղեղը (message/ad/page-ի համար — copy-ն՝ **Skill 08**)։ **TOFU/MOFU/BOFU**-ն՝ marketing-ի stage-ների քարտեզը (awareness/consideration/decision). ամեն asset-ը կապիր իր stage-ին։ **AARRR**-ն (McClure)՝ ամբողջ-կյանքի քարտեզը, որ Bro-ն default-ով instrument ա անում, որովհետև ներառում ա այն, ինչ AIDA/TOFU-ն բաց են թողնում՝ մնա՞ց, ավելի՞ վճարեց, ուրիշների՞ն բերեց։

### 2.2 AARRR in depth (instrument each stage, fix the worst one)
**Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral.** Instrument each stage, find the worst-converting step, fix *that one*. The biggest leak is the highest-leverage fix. Carry one rate per transition (not just stage counts) so you can see *where* the chain multiplies down.

- **Acquisition** — channels (organic/SEO, paid, outbound, partner/channel, referral, community, events). Track **cost-per-lead, lead→customer rate by channel, and channel quality** (do these leads retain?), not just volume. A cheap channel that never activates is expensive.
- **Activation** — the first real value moment (the **"aha"**). Get users there fast; **most churn is failure to activate**, not failure to retain. Define activation as a concrete, measurable event tied to delivered value (e.g. "invited a teammate AND completed one core action in 24h"), not "signed up." (Full treatment §6.4.)
- **Retention** — **cohort retention curves**. A curve that **flattens** above zero = product-market fit (a stable base that keeps coming back). A curve **to zero** = no fit yet — *do not scale acquisition; you'll just churn faster*. This curve is the single most important chart in the business.
- **Revenue** — conversion free→paid, ARPA/ARPU, expansion, contraction, pricing realization.
- **Referral** — turn happy customers into a loop (the bridge from funnel-thinking to loop-thinking — see §6).

**HY:** Instrument արա ամեն stage, գտի՛ր ամենավատ converting step-ը, ուղղի՛ր *հենց դա*։ Ամենամեծ ծակն ա ամենաբարձր-leverage-ի ուղղումը։ Կրի՛ր transition-ի rate (ոչ միայն stage-ի թիվ), որ տեսնես, թե *որտեղ* ա շղթան բազմապատկվում ներքև։ Activation = առաջին իրական value-պահը (aha-ն). churn-ի մեծ մասը activate չլինելն ա, ոչ թե retain չլինելը։ Cohort retention curve-ը, որ հարթվում ա զրոյից վեր = PMF. զրոյի գնացողը = դեռ fit չկա, մի՛ scale արա acquisition-ը։

### 2.3 The numbers that actually run the business
Know these cold; they are the unit-economics layer that keeps growth honest (deep treatment lives in **Skill 07**, but you reason with them constantly here):

- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** = fully-loaded sales+marketing spend ÷ new customers. *Fully-loaded* — include salaries, tools, not just ad spend. "Blended" CAC hides bad paid channels behind free organic; track **paid CAC** separately.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)** = gross-margin per customer × average lifetime (= 1 ÷ churn for recurring). **Use gross margin, not revenue** — LTV on revenue is a vanity number.
- **LTV:CAC** — health ratio. **~3:1 is the rule-of-thumb target.** <1 = you lose money on every customer (turn off the tap). 1–3 = thin, fix economics before scaling. **>5 may mean underinvesting** in growth (leaving the market to competitors).
- **CAC payback period** — months to recoup CAC from gross-margin contribution. **<12 months** is healthy for SMB SaaS; <18–24 tolerable for enterprise. This, more than LTV:CAC, governs how fast you can grow without burning cash, because it sets how long your money is tied up.
- **Churn (logo vs revenue)** — logo churn = customers lost; revenue churn = $ lost. They differ when you lose small accounts but keep big ones (or vice versa). **Gross revenue churn** = lost revenue ÷ starting; **net revenue churn** = (lost − expansion) ÷ starting.
- **NRR / NDR (Net Revenue/Dollar Retention)** = (start MRR + expansion − contraction − churn) ÷ start MRR, *existing customers only*. **>100% is the holy grail** (you grow with zero new logos); best-in-class SaaS 120%+. The clearest proof the product compounds inside accounts. **GRR (Gross Revenue Retention)** = (start − contraction − churn) ÷ start, *expansion excluded* — caps at 100%, and is the honest measure of the *floor* (how much you keep before any upsell heroics). NRR can hide a leaky base behind a few whales expanding; **read GRR and NRR together** (§7).
- **Magic Number** = net-new ARR ÷ prior-period S&M spend. >0.75 → sales efficiency is good, lean in; <0.5 → fix the motion before adding reps.
- **Sales velocity** = (# qualified opportunities × average deal value × win rate) ÷ sales-cycle length. The single equation of pipeline throughput — it names the four levers you can pull to make more money per unit time, and shows *cutting cycle length* and *raising win rate* often beat *adding leads*.
- **Win rate** = deals won ÷ deals worked (or ÷ qualified). Falling win rate with rising lead volume = a qualification problem upstream, not a closing problem (§2.5).
- **Rule of 40** — growth rate % + profit margin % ≥ 40 for a healthy growth-stage software company. Buys you a single number to trade growth against burn.
- **Quick Ratio** = (new MRR + expansion MRR) ÷ (churned + contraction MRR). >4 = strong, efficient growth; near 1 = treadmill (adding as fast as you leak).
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score — Reichheld)** = % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6) on "how likely are you to recommend us?" A *loyalty/referral leading indicator*, not a precise metric — use the trend and the verbatim "why," not the absolute number, and never tie comp to it (Goodhart's law gaming). A high NPS with low referral means you have goodwill you aren't harvesting into a loop (§6.1).

**HY:** Իմացի՛ր սրանք անգիր. unit-economics-ի շերտն են, որ growth-ը ազնիվ ա պահում (խորությունը՝ **Skill 07**, բայց այստեղ անընդհատ սրանցով ես մտածում)։ CAC = լրիվ-բեռնված S&M ÷ նոր customer-ներ (ներառիր աշխատավարձ, ոչ միայն ad-spend. paid CAC-ն առանձին հետևիր)։ LTV = gross-margin × կյանք (*margin*-ով, ոչ revenue-ով)։ LTV:CAC ~3:1 թիրախ։ CAC payback <12 ամիս SMB-ի, <18–24 enterprise-ի համար — սա ավելի շատ ա որոշում աճի արագությունը, քան LTV:CAC։ GRR-ն ու NRR-ը կարդա՛ միասին՝ NRR-ը կարող ա ծակ base թաքցնել մի քանի whale-ի expansion-ի հետևում։ Sales velocity-ն ա pipeline-ի թողունակության հավասարումը։ NPS-ն (Reichheld) loyalty-ի leading indicator ա՝ trend ու «ինչու», ոչ թե բացարձակ թիվ, ու երբեք comp-ին մի՛ կապիր։

### 2.4 Funnel diagnosis discipline
- **Find the leverage step:** for each transition compute rate × upstream volume × downstream value. Fix the one with the biggest *product* — the worst-converting step is not always at the lowest rate; a mediocre rate on a huge, high-value stage can outrank an awful rate on a tiny one.
- **Conversion ≠ cause.** A low close rate can be a *qualification* problem (bad leads upstream) masquerading as a *closing* problem. Trace leaks backward before retraining the team downstream.
- **Time-in-stage is a metric too.** Deals that sit are deals dying; instrument stage age and chase staleness, not just stage count.
- **Pipeline coverage & weighting.** Carry **pipeline coverage** (open pipeline ÷ quota, healthy ≈ 3×) and a **weighted forecast** (stage probability × deal value), but never let a CRM probability substitute for a real qualification read — happy-ears weighting forecasts deals that were never real.

**HY:** Գտի՛ր leverage step-ը՝ ամեն transition-ի համար rate × upstream volume × downstream value, ուղղի՛ր ամենամեծ *արտադրյալով*-ը (ամենավատ step-ը միշտ չի ամենացածր rate-ով)։ Conversion ≠ պատճառ. ցածր close-rate-ը կարող ա qualification-ի խնդիր լինել, ոչ թե closing-ի. հետևիր ծակին հետ։ Stage-ում մնացած ժամանակը metric ա. նստած deal-ը մեռնող deal ա։ Pipeline coverage ~3× ու weighted forecast կրի՛ր, բայց CRM-ի probability-ն իրական qualification-ի փոխարեն մի՛ դիր։

### 2.5 The funnel as a conversion-rate map (the diagnostic table)
Carry the canonical SaaS funnel and its *transition rates* in your head, so you spot which one is anomalous:

| Transition | What it measures | Where a low rate points |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → Lead/Signup | message-market fit, landing page | wrong promise, weak CTA, friction (Skill 03) |
| Lead → MQL/PQL | targeting + scoring quality | fuzzy ICP, scoring miscalibrated (§3, §5.1) |
| MQL/PQL → SQL/Opportunity | qualification, sales acceptance | bad leads, or sales ignoring marketing's leads |
| Opportunity → Closed-Won | discovery, value, deal execution | weak discovery, single-threading, no compelling event (§3) |
| Trial → Paid (PLG) | activation + time-to-value | aha not reached, onboarding friction (§6.4) |
| Customer → Renewal/Expansion | value realized, CS motion | poor onboarding, no QBR, wrong value metric (§4, §6.5) |

**Rule:** an anomalously low rate at *any* row is the highest-leverage place to spend — but trace it **upstream first** (a bad Opportunity→Won rate is often a Lead→MQL targeting failure wearing a closing-problem costume).

**HY:** Կրի՛ր canonical funnel-ը ու transition rate-երը գլխումդ, որ նկատես, թե որն ա anomal։ Ցանկացած տողի անոմալ ցածր rate-ը ամենաբարձր-leverage տեղն ա ծախսելու — բայց հետևիր **upstream-ին առաջինը**. վատ Opportunity→Won rate-ը հաճախ Lead→MQL targeting-ի ձախողում ա՝ closing-խնդրի կոստյումով։

## 3. SALES MOTION — taking a buyer from interest to signed

### 3.1 Qualification (do this first, ruthlessly)
- **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC** — **M**etrics (quantified value), **E**conomic buyer (who controls budget), **D**ecision criteria, **D**ecision process, **(P)**aper process (legal/procurement), **I**dentify pain, **C**hampion, **(C)**ompetition. The enterprise-deal gold standard: know *who decides, by what criteria, through what process, and who fights for you inside.* A deal missing the economic buyer or a real champion is not qualified, however nice the conversation.
- **BANT** — Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline. Lighter, fine for SMB/transactional; too thin for complex enterprise (it skips process and champion).
- **CHAMP / GPCTBA** — newer lightweight frames (CHAMP leads with **CH**allenges before authority; HubSpot's GPCTBA/C&I adds Goals/Plans/Timeline/Budget/Authority/Consequences&Implications). Use a *lighter* frame as ACV drops; the principle is constant — qualify on real pain, real money, real authority, real timeline.
- **Champion vs coach.** A *coach* gives you info; a *champion* spends their own political capital to sell internally when you're not in the room. Confirm you have a champion by testing: will they get you to the economic buyer? If not, you have a coach (useful, insufficient).
- **Disqualify on:** no economic buyer access, no compelling event/timeline, no budget reality, no fit. A clean disqualify frees the scarcest resource (rep time) for winnable deals.

**HY:** Qualify արա առաջինը, անխնա։ **MEDDIC/MEDDPICC**-ն enterprise-deal-ի ոսկե ստանդարտն ա՝ Metrics/Economic-buyer/Decision-criteria/Decision-process/Paper-process/Pain/Champion/Competition։ Economic buyer-ի կամ իրական champion-ի բացակայող deal-ը qualified չի, ինչքան էլ զրույցը հաճելի լինի։ BANT-ը թեթև ա (SMB), enterprise-ին բարակ։ ACV-ի անկման հետ վերցրու ավելի *թեթև* frame, սկզբունքը հաստատուն ա՝ իրական pain/money/authority/timeline։ **Champion**-ը իր քաղաքական կապիտալը ծախսում ա քեզ համար, երբ դու սենյակում չես. **coach**-ը պարզապես info ա տալիս։ Disqualify՝ economic-buyer-ի access չկա / compelling-event չկա / budget չկա / fit չկա։

### 3.2 Discovery — make the pain expensive before you sell the cure
- **SPIN (Rackham)** — **S**ituation → **P**roblem → **I**mplication → **N**eed-payoff. The art is **Implication**: make the *cost of the status quo* vivid and quantified ("so that delay costs ~X per month across Y teams?") *before* presenting the solution. People buy when the pain of staying exceeds the pain of changing. Rackham's research: in *large* sales, closing techniques and feature-dumping *hurt*; question quality (especially Implication & Need-payoff) is what separates top performers.
- **Challenger (Dixon & Adamson)** — Teach (reframe the buyer's thinking with a commercial insight), Tailor (to the stakeholder), Take control (of the process and the price conversation). Best in complex, consensus-driven B2B where the buyer doesn't already know what they need. The data: relationship-builders lose; challengers who teach win. Pairs with **Challenger Customer** (Dixon's sequel): in a committee, find and arm the **Mobilizer** (a skeptic who drives change), not the easy-to-talk-to "Talker."
- **Solution / Consultative selling** — diagnose before you prescribe; the rep is a trusted adviser who co-designs the answer, not a pitch-deck reader. The mother methodology under SPIN/Challenger: *no prescription without diagnosis* (selling a solution before you understand the problem is malpractice).
- **Value selling** — quantify the business case explicitly: build the **ROI / business case / value model** with the buyer (cost of the problem, value of the outcome, payback period of *your price*). Turns "it's expensive" into "it pays for itself in N months." The discipline that makes a price defensible to a CFO (cross-link **Skill 07** for the model math).
- **Sandler** — a buyer-seller "equal business stature" frame: an **up-front contract** (agree the agenda + possible outcomes, *including "no," up front*), pain-funnel questioning, and "negative reverse" to disqualify. Strong at *removing pressure* and surfacing the real "no" early — anti-happy-ears by design.
- **JTBD framing** — uncover the *job* the buyer is hiring the product to do, and the forces (push of the problem, pull of the new, anxiety, habit/inertia). Sell *toward* push+pull, *defuse* anxiety+inertia (that's what trials, guarantees, onboarding, and references are for).
- **Discovery rule:** talk-to-listen ratio should favor the buyer; the rep's job in discovery is questions, not pitch. A demo before discovery is a guess.

**HY:** **SPIN** (Rackham)՝ Situation→Problem→Implication→Need-payoff. արվեստը **Implication**-ն ա՝ status quo-ի *գինը* դարձրու վառ ու քանակական *լուծումը ներկայացնելուց առաջ*։ Rackham-ի հետազոտությունը՝ *մեծ* վաճառքում closing-techniques-ն ու feature-dump-ը *վնասում* են. հարցի որակն ա տարբերում top performer-ին։ **Challenger** (Dixon)՝ Teach/Tailor/Take-control. լավագույնը complex, consensus B2B-ում. committee-ում գտի՛ր ու զինի՛ր **Mobilizer**-ին, ոչ թե հաճելի «Talker»-ին։ **Solution/Consultative**՝ ախտորոշում նախքան դեղատոմս (խնդիրը չհասկանալով լուծում վաճառելը malpractice ա)։ **Value selling**՝ քանակականացրու business case-ը՝ «թանկ ա»-ն դարձրու «N ամսում ինքն իրեն ա մարում» (մաթեմը՝ **Skill 07**)։ **Sandler**՝ up-front contract, pain-funnel, վաղ «ոչ»-ը մակերես հանելը (anti-happy-ears)։

### 3.3 Running the deal
- **Multi-thread** — never single-thread on one contact; build champion + economic buyer + users + (in enterprise) the blocker/procurement. Single-threaded deals die when your one contact leaves, reorgs, or goes quiet. **Map the buying committee** (B2B average is many stakeholders, not one).
- **Mutual action plan (MAP) / close plan** — a shared, dated, working-backward-from-go-live plan co-owned with the champion. Converts "hope it closes" into a tracked process and surfaces hidden steps (security review, legal, procurement) early.
- **Handle objections** — surface them early; address the *real* concern (price objections are usually *unclear value* objections — they don't see enough ROI yet). Don't argue; isolate ("if we solved X, is there anything else?"), then resolve. The objection you don't surface kills the deal silently in committee. (Negotiation craft — anchoring, trades, never split the difference — lives in **Skill 08**; here it's *deal mechanics*, there it's *technique*.)
- **Compelling event** — a dated business reason to act now (contract renewal, deadline, mandate, pain milestone). No compelling event → no urgency → the deal slips forever. Manufacture legitimate urgency from the buyer's own calendar, never fake scarcity.
- **Close** — every interaction ends with a clear, dated **next step**. Ask for the decision directly. Trial-close throughout ("if this works in the pilot, what would stop us going company-wide?") so the final ask is a formality, not a surprise.
- **Forecast honestly.** Commit / Best-case / Pipeline categories should reflect a real qualification read (MEDDIC complete, MAP on track), not optimism. A pretty forecast built on happy ears is the most expensive lie in a sales org.

**HY:** **Multi-thread** արա միշտ՝ champion + economic-buyer + users + procurement. single-threaded deal-ը մեռնում ա, երբ քո մեկ կոնտակտը հեռանում/reorg/լռում ա. քարտեզագրի՛ր buying committee-ն։ **MAP** (mutual action plan)՝ ընդհանուր, ամսաթվերով, go-live-ից հետ աշխատող պլան champion-ի հետ — թաքնված քայլերը (security/legal/procurement) վաղ մակերես ա հանում։ Objection-ը հանի՛ր վաղ ու լուծիր *իրական* մտահոգությունը (price-objection-ը սովորաբար *անհասկանալի-value* ա). bանակցության techniqueն՝ **Skill 08**։ **Compelling event** = ամսաթվով պատճառ՝ հիմա գործելու. առանց դրա deal-ը հավերժ սահում ա։ Ամեն փոխազդեցություն ավարտվում ա պարզ, ամսաթվով **հաջորդ քայլով**։ Forecast արա ազնիվ՝ qualification-ի իրական ընթերցմամբ, ոչ թե optimism-ով։

### 3.4 The motion taxonomy (who/what does the selling)
Motion = the *primary mechanism* that moves a buyer from awareness to revenue. Five archetypes; most companies run a **hybrid** but should know which is the *engine* and which is the *assist*.

| Motion | Primary engine | Fits | The trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product-led (PLG)** | the product itself acquires/activates/converts/expands; humans assist | low ACV, fast time-to-value, individual/team adoption, broad market, self-evident value | monetizing the free tier; PLG with no aha = a leaky free pool |
| **Sales-led (SLG)** | reps drive the deal (inside or field) | high ACV, committees, custom terms, non-obvious value | burning rep salaries on deals too small to pay for them |
| **Marketing-led** | demand-gen / content / inbound creates and nurtures most pipeline | considered purchases, content-rich categories, mid ACV | MQL volume that sales won't accept; pipeline that doesn't convert |
| **Community-led** | a community (users, developers, advocates) drives awareness, trust, and adoption | developer tools, open-source, prosumer, network/identity products | community as a tactic, not a system; extracting without giving |
| **Channel/partner-led** | partners resell or refer; their distribution is the engine | enterprise reach, geographic/vertical expansion, ecosystems | trading margin for reach you could have built; channel conflict |

- **Choosing by ACV/segment:** the **ACV ladder** — roughly, **<$1k self-serve/PLG**, **$1k–$15k inside sales (+ PLG top-of-funnel)**, **$15k–$100k inside + light field**, **>$100k field/enterprise + channel**. The motion follows the math: a human's fully-loaded cost sets the floor ACV that can profitably support touch.
- **Hybrid / product-led sales (PLS):** the durable end-state for most. PLG fills the top and lands accounts cheaply (bottom-up); sales expands them upmarket (top-down). Design the **handoff**: a **product-qualified lead (PQL)** — a user whose in-product behavior signals buying intent (hit a usage limit, invited 5 teammates, used the core feature 10×) — beats a marketing-qualified lead (MQL) for conversion. The org design (when sales touches a self-serve account) is the make-or-break.
- **Inside vs field within SLG:** inside = remote close, mid ACV, demos + short cycles, higher rep capacity; field = high ACV, long cycles, on-site, AEs + SEs + CS. Most modern motions are **inside-first**, field only where ACV demands it.

**HY:** Motion = *հիմնական մեխանիզմը*, որ գնորդին awareness-ից revenue ա տանում։ Հինգ archetype, ընկերությունների մեծ մասը **hybrid** ա, բայց պետք ա իմանա՝ որն ա *engine*-ը, որը՝ *assist*-ը։ **PLG**՝ product-ն ինքն ա acquire/activate/convert/expand անում (ցածր ACV, արագ TTV). **SLG**՝ rep-երն են deal-ը տանում (բարձր ACV, committee). **Marketing-led**՝ demand-gen/content-ն ա pipeline-ը ստեղծում. **Community-led**՝ community-ն ա awareness/trust-ը (dev-tools, open-source). **Channel-led**՝ partner-ների distribution-ն ա engine-ը։ **ACV-ի սանդուղքով** ընտրիր՝ <$1k self-serve, $1k–15k inside, >$100k field+channel — motion-ը հետևում ա մաթեմին։ Durable վերջնավիճակը **product-led sales**-ն ա՝ PLG-ն land ա անում, sales-ը expand. design արա **handoff**-ը՝ PQL-ն (in-product վարքով intent) գերակա ա MQL-ից։

## 4. PRICING & PACKAGING — the strongest, least-used lever

### 4.1 Foundations
- **Value-based, not cost-plus, not competitor-copy.** Price to the value delivered / the customer's best alternative, not your cost. Cost-plus leaves money on the table; copying competitors imports their mistakes. **Design the price *with* the product, not after it** (Ramanujam & Tacke: "monetizing innovation" means asking willingness-to-pay *before* you build).
- **WTP first.** Willingness-to-pay is a fact about the customer you can research, not a guess you make at launch. Find it early; it shapes what you even build.
- **Price discrimination is legitimate and necessary.** Different segments have different WTP; capturing it (via tiers, fences, geography, usage, seats, time) is not gouging — a single price either leaves the high-WTP segment's money on the table or prices out the low-WTP segment. The whole packaging craft (§4.2) is *structured, ethical price discrimination*.

**HY:** **Value-based**, ոչ cost-plus, ոչ competitor-copy։ Գնագոյացրու ըստ delivered value-ի / գնորդի լավագույն այլընտրանքի, ոչ թե քո cost-ի։ Design արա գինը product-ի *հետ*, ոչ թե հետո (Ramanujam & Tacke՝ WTP հարցրու *նախքան* կառուցելը)։ **WTP**-ն փաստ ա, որ կարող ես research անել, ոչ թե guess։ **Price discrimination**-ը օրինաչափ ու անհրաժեշտ ա. տարբեր segment-ներ տարբեր WTP ունեն. մեկ գինը կամ թողնում ա բարձր-WTP-ի փողը սեղանին, կամ դուրս ա մղում ցածր-WTP-ին։ Ողջ packaging-ը *կառուցված, էթիկ price discrimination* ա։

### 4.2 The three pricing decisions (each is separate)
1. **Pricing model / value metric** — *what you charge for*. Pick the axis that **scales with the value the customer receives** (seats, usage/consumption, transactions, outcomes, tiers). A good value metric grows revenue automatically as the customer succeeds, aligns price with perceived value, and makes expansion frictionless. **Bad value metric = the #1 pricing mistake** — if it doesn't track value, you either cap your upside or punish your best customers. Test: "does a customer who gets 2× the value pay roughly 2×?" **Seat vs usage:** seat-based is simple, predictable, easy to budget — but caps with headcount and can punish broad adoption; usage-based (consumption) scales with value and expands automatically — but is harder to forecast and can shock the buyer. Hybrid (platform fee + usage, or seats + usage overage) is increasingly the norm.
2. **Packaging / segmentation** — *how you bundle and fence*. **Good/Better/Best tiers** to capture different WTP segments; **anchor** with a premium tier (makes the middle look reasonable — the decoy/compromise effect); fence features so each segment self-selects into its tier. Three tiers is the default (most pick the middle); a fourth "enterprise: call us" tier captures the top without a public number. **Fence on a dimension the buyer self-sorts by** (volume, seats, support level, advanced features, compliance/SSO), never on something that feels arbitrary or punitive.
3. **Price level & structure** — *the actual numbers and shape*. Charm pricing, round vs precise (precise = "we calculated this"; round = "premium"), annual-vs-monthly (discount annual to pull cash forward and cut churn), per-unit vs platform fee + usage.

**HY:** Երեք առանձին որոշում։ **(1) Value metric** — *ինչի* համար ես գանձում. ընտրիր առանցքը, որ scale ա ստացված value-ի հետ (seat/usage/transaction/outcome)։ Վատ value metric-ը **#1 սխալն ա**։ Test՝ «2× value ստացողը ~2× վճարո՞ւմ ա»։ Seat-based՝ պարզ, կանխատեսելի, բայց headcount-ով սահմանափակ. usage-based՝ scale ա value-ով, ինքնաշխատ expand, բայց դժվար forecast. hybrid-ն ա norm դառնում։ **(2) Packaging** — Good/Better/Best, premium **anchor**-ով (decoy effect), feature-ները fence արա այնպիսի dimension-ով, որ գնորդը ինքն ա self-sort անում (volume/seats/support/SSO)։ **(3) Price level & structure** — charm, round vs precise, annual discount՝ cash-ը առաջ քաշելու ու churn կտրելու։

### 4.3 Researching the number
- **van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter (PSM)** — four questions (too cheap / cheap / expensive / too expensive) → an acceptable price *range* and the optimal point. Good for a sanity range, weak on revenue maximization.
- **Gabor-Granger** — ask purchase intent at successive price points → demand curve + revenue-maximizing price. Better for finding the actual number.
- **Conjoint analysis** — buyers choose among feature/price bundles → reveals which features carry WTP and how to package. Best for tier design.
- **The cheapest research is selling.** A/B price tests, sales-floor signal (where deals stall on price), win/loss interviews, and the discount you're forced to give all reveal real WTP. Research informs; the market decides.

**HY:** **van Westendorp PSM**՝ չորս հարց (շատ-էժան/էժան/թանկ/շատ-թանկ) → ընդունելի *միջակայք* + optimal կետ (լավ sanity-range, թույլ revenue-max-ի)։ **Gabor-Granger**՝ purchase-intent հաջորդական գներով → demand curve + revenue-max գին (ավելի լավ՝ իրական թիվ գտնելու)։ **Conjoint**՝ feature/price bundle-ների ընտրություն → որ feature-ն ա WTP կրում (լավագույնը tier-design-ի)։ Ամենաէժան research-ը *վաճառելն* ա՝ A/B price-test, sales-floor-ի signal, win/loss հարցազրույց, ստիպված discount-ը։

### 4.4 Expansion & dynamics
- **Expansion built in (land-and-expand)** — design upgrade paths so accounts grow: usage tiers that fill up, seats that spread, features that get adopted then upsold. **NRR > 100% is a packaging decision as much as a product one** — the value metric *is* the expansion engine.
- **Freemium vs free-trial (the front-door decision):** **free trial** = time-boxed full (or near-full) value → urgency + qualification, converts faster, fits when value is *fast and obvious*; **freemium** = perpetual limited tier → top-of-funnel volume + a standing surface for a loop (virality, data, network), but harder to monetize and risks cannibalizing paid — **only choose freemium if the free tier itself drives a loop** or is your distribution. **Reverse trial** (start in full-featured trial, drop to free at expiry) blends both.
- **Grandfather thoughtfully on price increases** — you can raise prices for new customers and at renewal; surprise increases on loyal customers churn them. Communicate value before price.
- **Discount discipline** — discounting is borrowing against future margin and training buyers to wait. **Never give a discount for nothing**; trade it for term length, prepayment, references, case studies, or expansion commitment. A list price you never hold is not a price.

**HY:** **Land-and-expand**՝ design արա upgrade-ուղիներ (usage-tier-ները լցվում են, seat-երը տարածվում, feature-ները adopt-վում ապա upsell)։ **NRR > 100%-ը packaging-ի որոշում ա** ոնց product-ի. value metric-ն *ինքն* ա expansion engine-ը։ **Freemium vs free-trial**՝ trial = ժամանակով-սահմանված լրիվ value (urgency+qualification, արագ convert, երբ value-ն արագ ու ակնհայտ ա). freemium = հավերժ սահմանափակ tier (top-funnel volume + loop-ի մակերես), բայց դժվար monetize — **ընտրիր freemium միայն եթե free tier-ն ինքն ա loop քշում**։ Price-increase-ը grandfather արա խելացի. discount-ը երբեք *ոչնչի դիմաց*՝ trade արա term/prepay/reference/expansion-ի դիմաց։

## 5. DEMAND GENERATION & GO-TO-MARKET

### 5.1 ICP and positioning come first
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** — the *segment* (firmographics + the specific job/pain) where you win fastest and retain best. Everything (channel, message, motion, price) is downstream of ICP. A fuzzy ICP makes every channel underperform.
- **Positioning (April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome*)** — context-setting in five steps: (1) competitive **alternatives** (what the customer would do without you, including "nothing/spreadsheet") → (2) your unique **attributes** → (3) the **value** those enable → (4) the **customers** (the *who*) who care most about that value → (5) the **market category** you choose to frame yourself in (and the trends that make it urgent). Positioning is a *deliberate choice*, not a description; pick the frame where your strengths are the buying criteria. **Weak positioning is the silent killer** — a good product framed in the wrong category loses to a worse product framed right.
- **Category design (*Play Bigger* — Lochhead, Ramadan)** — when no existing category flatters you, *create* one: name the problem, name the new category, and condition the market to want it (the "category king" captures the majority of the category's economics). High-risk, high-reward; most companies should *position within* a category (Dunford) before they try to *design* one. Don't category-design to dodge the harder work of just being clearly better in an existing frame.
- **Value proposition (the artifact)** — the one-line promise + the proof, often built on a **Value Proposition Canvas** (jobs/pains/gains ↔ products/pain-relievers/gain-creators) so the offer maps to the JTBD. **Message-market fit** — the headline that makes the right buyer lean in. Test it in ads and headlines before scaling spend (copy craft → **Skill 08**).

**HY:** **ICP**-ն ա առաջինը՝ *segment*-ը (firmographic + կոնկրետ job/pain), որտեղ ամենաարագ ես շահում ու լավ retain անում. ամեն ինչ (channel/message/motion/price) ICP-ի downstream ա։ **Positioning** (Dunford, *Obviously Awesome*)՝ հինգ քայլ՝ competitive alternatives → unique attributes → value → who cares → market category։ Positioning-ը *որոշում* ա, ոչ թե նկարագրություն. ընտրիր frame-ը, որտեղ քո ուժերը գնման-չափանիշ են։ **Category design** (*Play Bigger*)՝ երբ existing category չի շոյում քեզ, *ստեղծիր* նորը (category king-ը գրավում ա economics-ի մեծ մասը) — բարձր ռիսկ, մեծ մասը պետք ա նախ *position անի* category-ի ներսում։ **Value proposition**՝ մեկ-տողանի խոստում + ապացույց (Value Proposition Canvas-ով JTBD-ին map արած)։ Copy-ն՝ **Skill 08**։

### 5.2 Channels & channel-product fit
- **The channel must match how the product is discovered and bought.** Not every channel fits every product: high-consideration enterprise rarely converts on impulse social ads; a self-serve tool may die in a long outbound cycle. (Pirate playbook / *Traction*'s **Bullseye**: brainstorm all channels → test a few → focus on the one or two that work, then exploit.)
- **One channel usually dominates** at a given stage. Resist spreading thin across ten; find the bullseye, saturate it, *then* diversify when it caps.
- **Channel taxonomy:** organic (SEO, content, community), paid (search, social, display, retargeting), outbound (cold email/calls, **ABM** for enterprise), product/viral (referral, network effects, embeds), partner/channel, events, PR. Map each to the funnel stage it serves and to a payback target.
- **Inbound vs outbound vs PLG, by motion:** **inbound/content/SEO** compounds (a loop, §6.1) but is slow to start; **outbound/ABM** is controllable and fast but linear (a tap — stops when reps stop); **paid** is instant and scalable but only a loop if LTV funds the next cohort; **PLG/product-virality** is the cheapest at scale but needs a product that pulls. Match the channel to the motion (§3.4) and the math (§2.3) — the same channel is a loop for one company and a tap for another.

**HY:** Channel-ը պետք ա համապատասխանի, թե ոնց ա product-ը հայտնաբերվում ու գնվում. ամեն channel ամեն product-ին չի սազում (*Traction*-ի **Bullseye**՝ brainstorm բոլորը → test մի քանիսը → կենտրոնացիր աշխատողի վրա → exploit)։ Մեկ channel սովորաբար գերակշռում ա տվյալ stage-ում. մի՛ ցրվիր տասի վրա՝ saturate արա bullseye-ն, *հետո* diversify։ Taxonomy՝ organic/paid/outbound(ABM)/viral/partner/events/PR՝ ամեն մեկը կապիր funnel-stage-ին ու payback-target-ին։ **Inbound/content**՝ compound-վում ա (loop), դանդաղ սկսում. **outbound/ABM**՝ արագ բայց գծային (tap). **paid**՝ ակնթարթ բայց loop ա միայն եթե LTV-ն հաջորդ cohort-ն ա ֆինանսավորում. **PLG**՝ ամենաէժան scale-ում։

### 5.3 Diffusion & timing (Moore + Bass)
- **Crossing the Chasm (Moore)** — the gap between **early adopters** (buy vision, tolerate rough edges) and the **early majority** (buy proven solutions with references). The chasm kills startups that win innovators then assume the mainstream will follow. **Cross it with a beachhead:** dominate one narrow, well-referenced segment completely (the "bowling pin"), then knock down adjacent ones. Don't try to serve everyone before you own someone. (The five adopter categories — innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, laggards — are Rogers' *Diffusion of Innovations*; Moore added the chasm.)
- **Bass diffusion model** — adoption = innovation (external/marketing-driven, coefficient *p*) + imitation (word-of-mouth, internal, coefficient *q*). Tells you when growth is loop-driven (imitation *q* high) vs spend-driven (innovation *p* high) — and therefore where to invest. A high *q* is the quantitative fingerprint of a working viral/word-of-mouth loop (§6.1).

**HY:** **Crossing the Chasm** (Moore)՝ վիհը **early adopter**-ների (vision են գնում) ու **early majority**-ի (ապացուցված, reference-ով) միջև. chasm-ը սպանում ա startup-ին, որ innovator-ներին շահում ա ապա ենթադրում mainstream-ը կհետևի։ Անցի՛ր **beachhead**-ով՝ լրիվ տիրիր մեկ նեղ, լավ-reference-ով segment-ին («bowling pin»), հետո հարակիցները։ Adopter-ի հինգ կատեգորիան Rogers-ինն ա. chasm-ը՝ Moore-ի։ **Bass model**՝ adoption = innovation (*p*, marketing) + imitation (*q*, word-of-mouth). բարձր *q* = աշխատող viral loop-ի քանակական մատնահետք։

## 6. GROWTH — building the compounding engine

### 6.0 The growth model & North Star (the operating frame — Ellis/Balfour)
Before loops and experiments, write the **growth model**: the *equation* that decomposes your North Star into the inputs you actually move. North Star = f(acquisition × activation × retention × monetization × referral). Sean Ellis (who coined "growth hacking" and the PMF test) and Brian Balfour (Reforge) frame growth as a *system with a model*, not a bag of tricks — you can only manage what you can model.

- **North Star Metric (NSM)** — the single durable leading indicator of **delivered customer value** that, if it grows, the business grows (e.g. "weekly active teams collaborating," "nights booked," "messages sent between connected users"). It is *value delivered*, not revenue (revenue is a lagging *result*; the NSM is the *cause*). The whole org aligns to it.
- **The growth model** — express the NSM as a formula of its inputs, attach a current number and a lever to each, and you have a map of where growth can come from. This turns "we need to grow" into "moving activation from 30%→40% adds X to the NSM, and here are the three experiments that target it."
- **Input metrics over output metrics.** You can't directly move revenue; you *can* move the inputs (signups, activation rate, retention curve, expansion rate). Manage the inputs (Balfour); the output follows.

**HY:** Loop-ից ու experiment-ից առաջ գրի՛ր **growth model**-ը՝ *հավասարումը*, որ քո North Star-ը քայքայում ա այն input-ների, որ իրականում շարժում ես։ NSM = f(acquisition × activation × retention × monetization × referral)։ Ellis-ը (ով հնարեց «growth hacking»-ն ու PMF-test-ը) ու Balfour-ը (Reforge) growth-ը frame են անում որպես *model-ով system*, ոչ թե հնարքների պարկ — կարող ես կառավարել միայն այն, ինչ կարող ես modelավորել։ **NSM**-ն *delivered value*-ի մեկ leading indicator ա (ոչ revenue. revenue-ն lagging *արդյունք* ա, NSM-ը՝ *պատճառ*)։ Շարժի՛ր **input**-ները (signup/activation/retention/expansion), output-ը կհետևի։

### 6.1 Loops, not funnels
- **Growth loops > linear funnels.** A funnel is one-way and one-shot: spend → output, then spend again. A **loop** reinvests its output as next-cycle input — each cohort's action generates the next cohort's acquisition. **Compounding beats one-shot campaigns.** Types:
  - **Viral / network loop** — users invite users (the product is better with more people, or users bring others to collaborate). Quantify with the **viral coefficient (k)** = invites sent × conversion rate; **k > 1 = self-sustaining viral growth** (rare, prize it); k < 1 still lowers blended CAC. Watch **cycle time** — fast sub-1 loops beat slow super-1 dreams. Distinguish **inherent virality** (product needs others to work — collaboration, payments) from **incentivized/word-of-mouth** virality (a referral program bolted on).
  - **Content loop** — users/usage generate content (UGC, SEO pages, reviews) → content acquires new users → who generate more content (e.g. marketplaces, review sites, community Q&A). The compounding engine behind most durable SEO.
  - **Paid loop** — revenue from cohort N funds acquisition of cohort N+1; sustainable only if **CAC payback < the reinvestment cycle** and LTV:CAC holds. This is a loop only if the math compounds, otherwise it's just a tap.
  - **Sales loop** — happy customers → references/case studies/word-of-mouth → warmer, cheaper pipeline → more happy customers. The bridge from §6.5 (CS) back to §5 (demand).
- **Design the loop explicitly:** state the step where output becomes input, instrument that step, and optimize *cycle time* and *conversion per turn* — loops compound on both. A loop with a great conversion-per-turn but a slow cycle time loses to a mediocre fast one.

**HY:** **Growth loop > գծային funnel։** Funnel-ը մեկ-ուղղությամբ ու մեկ-կրակոց ա. **loop**-ը իր output-ը վերաներդնում ա որպես հաջորդ-ցիկլի input — ամեն cohort-ի գործողությունը հաջորդ cohort-ի acquisition-ն ա ծնում։ **Compounding-ը գերակա ա մեկ-կրակոց campaign-ից։** Տեսակները՝ **viral/network** (k = invites × conversion. k>1 = ինքնապահպանվող, հազվադեպ. դիտի՛ր cycle-time-ը. տարբերիր *inherent* virality-ն incentivized-ից), **content** (usage → content → SEO → users), **paid** (cohort N-ի revenue-ն ֆինանսավորում ա N+1-ը. loop ա միայն եթե payback < cycle), **sales** (happy customers → reference → ավելի էժան pipeline)։ Design արա loop-ը *բացահայտ*՝ նշիր որտեղ ա output-ը input դառնում, optimize արա *cycle time* ու *conversion-per-turn*։

### 6.2 Focus & experimentation
- **One Metric That Matters (OMTM)** — pick the single metric that best proxies real value delivered *at the current stage*, and focus the whole team on it for the period. It changes as the company matures (activation → retention → expansion). Prevents the team from optimizing six things and moving none. (Distinct from the **North Star** of §6.0: the NSM is the *constant* the levers serve; the OMTM is the *current* lever.)
- **The growth experiment loop** — **hypothesis → prioritize → test → measure → keep/kill → systematize.** Prioritize with **ICE** (Impact × Confidence × Ease) or **PIE/RICE**; ship many cheap tests; **most experiments fail** — the discipline is killing fast and compounding the few wins. Run *velocity* of validated learning as a meta-metric (the Ellis high-tempo testing rhythm: a weekly cadence of proposing, prioritizing, running, and learning from experiments).
- **Statistical honesty** — call winners on adequate sample and significance, not on day-2 noise; beware peeking, novelty effects, and seasonal confounds. A "win" that doesn't survive a holdout was a fluctuation. (Experiment-design rigor is shared with **Skill 11** — analysis — and **Skill 13** — testing/quality.)

**HY:** **OMTM**՝ ընտրիր մեկ metric, որ լավագույնս proxy ա *ընթացիկ stage*-ի delivered value-ին, ողջ team-ը կենտրոնացրու դրա վրա period-ի համար (փոխվում ա հասունության հետ՝ activation→retention→expansion. տարբեր ա North Star-ից՝ NSM-ը *հաստատունն* ա, OMTM-ը՝ *ընթացիկ lever*-ը)։ **Experiment loop**՝ hypothesis → prioritize (ICE/RICE) → test → measure → keep/kill → systematize. *շատ* էժան test, *մեծ մասը ձախողվում ա*, կարգապահությունը արագ սպանելն ու քչերը compound անելն ա (Ellis-ի high-tempo testing-ի ռիթմը)։ **Statistical honesty**՝ winner հայտարարիր բավարար sample-ով, ոչ թե 2-րդ-օրվա աղմուկով (rigor-ը՝ **Skill 11/13**)։

### 6.3 Retention & expansion as growth
- **Retention is growth.** Improving retention lifts LTV, NRR, viral cycle (more users staying to invite), and payback simultaneously — it's the highest-leverage growth work and the most ignored because it's unglamorous. The **retention-is-the-foundation** principle: with bad retention, every other lever (acquisition, virality, monetization) is pouring water into a cracked vessel; with great retention, every other lever compounds.
- **Cohort curves & the three retention horizons** — read retention as **N-day** (does the product *stick* early — activation health), **week/month** (engagement habit), and **dollar/logo over quarters** (the business floor). A curve that drops then **flattens (the "smile" / plateau)** is the signal of PMF; the *height* of the plateau is the ceiling on everything else.
- **Activation is the leverage point for retention** (full treatment §6.4).
- **Resurrection & expansion loops** — winning back churned/dormant users and expanding existing accounts are usually cheaper than net-new acquisition; instrument and own them, don't leave them to chance.

**HY:** **Retention-ը growth ա։** Retention-ի բարելավումը միաժամանակ բարձրացնում ա LTV, NRR, viral cycle ու payback — ամենաբարձր-leverage-ի աշխատանքն ա ու ամենաանտեսվածը (ոչ-փայլուն ա)։ **retention-is-the-foundation**՝ վատ retention-ով ամեն մյուս lever-ը ճեղքված անոթ ա լցնում. լավ retention-ով ամեն lever compound-վում ա։ Cohort curve-ը կարդա երեք հորիզոնով՝ **N-day** (վաղ stick), **week/month** (habit), **dollar/logo եռամսյակներով** (բիզնեսի հատակ)։ Curve, որ ընկնում ապա **հարթվում ա (smile/plateau)** = PMF-ի signal. plateau-ի *բարձրությունն* ա ամեն մնացածի առաստաղը։

### 6.4 Activation, time-to-value & onboarding (the highest-leverage point)
Most churn is born here, not at month 6. Win activation and the whole engine lifts.

- **The aha-moment** — the first time the user *experiences* the core value (not "saw the dashboard" — *got the thing they came for*). Find it empirically: the in-product action that best separates retained from churned cohorts (e.g. Facebook's "7 friends in 10 days," Slack's "2,000 messages sent," Dropbox's "one file in one folder on one device"). Then engineer the whole onboarding to drive users to *that action, fast*.
- **Time-to-value (TTV)** — the elapsed time from signup to aha. **Shorten it relentlessly**; every step, field, and delay before value is a leak. The fastest path to the first win beats the most complete tour. Prefer **"do, then explain"** (let them get a win, then teach) over a long upfront tutorial.
- **Onboarding design** — reduce setup friction (sane defaults, templates, sample data, progressive disclosure), show progress (a checklist/setup score), and remove every non-essential step before the first value. **The empty state is the most important screen** — a blank dashboard is a dead end; seed it. (Onboarding *UX* craft is **Skill 03**; here it's the *growth lever*.)
- **Engagement / habit loops** — after activation, build the trigger → action → reward → investment cycle (Eyal's *Hooked*) and the right notification/lifecycle messaging so the product earns a place in the user's routine. Retention is a *habit*, and habits are built by loops, not features.
- **Set-up vs ongoing value** — distinguish the one-time "got it working" aha from the recurring "this keeps paying off" value that drives long-term retention. A product can nail activation and still churn if there's no recurring reason to return.

**HY:** Churn-ի մեծ մասը *այստեղ* ա ծնվում, ոչ թե 6-րդ ամսին։ **Aha-moment**՝ առաջին անգամ, երբ user-ը *ապրում* ա core value-ն (ոչ թե «տեսավ dashboard-ը» — *ստացավ այն, ինչի համար եկել ա*). գտի՛ր էմպիրիկ՝ in-product գործողությունը, որ լավագույնս տարբերում ա retained/churned cohort-ները (FB-ի «7 ընկեր 10 օրում», Slack-ի «2000 message»)։ **TTV**՝ signup-ից aha ընկած ժամանակը — **անխնա կրճատիր**. ամեն քայլ value-ից առաջ ծակ ա. «արա, հետո բացատրիր» > երկար tutorial։ **Onboarding**՝ կրճատիր friction (default, template, sample data, progressive disclosure), ցույց տուր progress, **empty state-ն ամենակարևոր էկրանն ա** — սերմանի՛ր այն (UX-ը՝ **Skill 03**)։ **Habit loop**՝ trigger→action→reward→investment (Eyal, *Hooked*). retention-ը *սովորություն* ա, սովորությունը՝ loop-ով, ոչ feature-ով։ Տարբերիր մեկանգամյա «աշխատեց» aha-ն կրկնվող «շարունակում ա մարել» value-ից։

### 6.5 Customer success & expansion (post-sale revenue)
Once acquisition is solved, the *cheapest* revenue is in the base. CS is a *revenue function*, not a cost center.

- **Onboarding → adoption → value-realization → renewal → expansion** — the CS lifecycle. The renewal is *earned in onboarding*, not at the renewal date; a customer who never reached value will not renew however good the QBR.
- **GRR vs NRR as the CS scoreboard** — **GRR** (gross retention, expansion excluded) measures whether you *keep* what you land (the floor; <90% annual is a leaky base). **NRR** (expansion included) measures whether the base *grows itself*; >100% is the compounding goal. CS owns GRR (stop churn) and co-owns NRR (drive expansion). Read both — strong NRR masking weak GRR means a few accounts expand while many quietly churn (§2.3).
- **Churn prevention** — instrument **health scores** (usage depth/breadth, key-user engagement, support sentiment, exec sponsor presence) to catch at-risk accounts *before* renewal; intervene on leading indicators (usage decline, champion departure, ticket spikes), not the lagging cancellation. **Churn is a process failure with a timestamp weeks earlier.**
- **QBRs / business reviews** — periodically prove delivered ROI to the economic buyer (re-justify the spend, re-anchor on outcomes, surface expansion). A QBR that's a feature tour wastes the meeting; tie it to *their* metrics and the value model (§3.2 value selling).
- **Land-and-expand execution** — map the account's whitespace (other teams, use-cases, products), and expand on the value metric you priced for (§4.4). Expansion to an existing happy account is the lowest-CAC revenue you have.
- **Logo retention vs net-dollar** — celebrate keeping *strategic* logos (references, network nodes) even at flat dollars; don't let a healthy NRR number hide the loss of a marquee account that anchored your category credibility.

**HY:** Acquisition-ը լուծելուց հետո ամենաէժան revenue-ն *base*-ի մեջ ա։ CS-ը *revenue function* ա, ոչ cost center։ Lifecycle՝ onboarding→adoption→value-realization→renewal→expansion. renewal-ը *վաստակվում ա onboarding-ում*, ոչ թե renewal-ի ամսաթվին։ **GRR**-ը (expansion-ից զատ) չափում ա՝ *պահո՞ւմ* ես land-ածդ (հատակ. <90%/տարի ծակ base ա). **NRR**-ը՝ base-ն ինքն իրեն *աճո՞ւմ ա*. երկուսն էլ կարդա։ **Churn prevention**՝ health score (usage-ի խորություն/լայնություն, key-user, sponsor), միջամտիր *leading* indicator-ին (usage-ի անկում, champion-ի հեռացում), ոչ թե lagging cancellation-ին — churn-ը process-ի ձախողում ա շաբաթներ առաջ timestamp-ով։ **QBR**՝ ապացուցիր delivered ROI economic-buyer-ին (նրա metric-ով, ոչ feature-tour)։ **Land-and-expand**՝ քարտեզագրի՛ր whitespace-ը, expand արա value-metric-ով՝ ամենացածր-CAC-ով revenue-ն։

### 6.6 The PMF gate (don't scale before it)
- **Sean Ellis PMF test** — survey users "how would you feel if you could no longer use this?"; **>40% "very disappointed" ≈ product-market fit**, and a green light to scale acquisition. Below that, fix the product before the funnel. Pair it with the **retention-curve flattening** (§6.3) and **organic/word-of-mouth pull** as the triangulated PMF signal — no single metric is PMF alone.
- **The gate rule:** scaling acquisition *before* PMF just churns faster and burns the cash you'll need *after* PMF. The hardest discipline in growth is *not* pressing the acquisition pedal until the engine holds water.

**HY:** **Sean Ellis PMF-test**՝ survey «ո՞նց կզգաս, եթե այլևս չկարողանաս սա օգտագործել». **>40% «շատ հիասթափված» ≈ PMF** ու կանաչ լույս acquisition scale անելու։ Ներքև — ուղղի՛ր product-ը funnel-ից առաջ։ Զուգի՛ր retention-curve-ի հարթման (§6.3) ու organic-pull-ի հետ՝ triangulated signal (ոչ մի մետրիկ մենակ PMF չի)։ **Gate-ի կանոնը**՝ PMF-ից *առաջ* acquisition scale անելը պարզապես ավելի արագ ա churn անում ու այրում cash-ը, որ պետք կգա PMF-ից *հետո*։

## 7. DECISION RULES (fast, reusable)

- **Pour in more traffic or fix conversion?** Fix the worst-converting funnel step first; then scale traffic into a tighter funnel. Traffic last, not first.
- **Should we scale acquisition at all?** Only if the cohort retention curve **flattens above zero**, PMF is triangulated (Ellis >40% + retention plateau + organic pull), and LTV:CAC clears ~3 with payback under your cash horizon. A curve to zero means scaling = churning faster.
- **Which GTM motion?** Follow the ACV ladder: tiny ACV → **PLG/self-serve** (humans can't touch it profitably); mid → **inside sales** (often PLG top-of-funnel); high ACV / committees → **field/enterprise + channel**; content-rich considered purchase → **marketing-led**; dev/prosumer/network product → **community-led**. Best durable end-state for most → **product-led sales** (PLG lands, sales expands; PQL > MQL).
- **Pursue this deal?** Qualify on fit + value + winnability (MEDDIC: economic buyer + champion + compelling event). Disqualify fast if any fails — a fast no beats a slow maybe.
- **Single rep, multi contact?** Always multi-thread; if you have one contact and can't get a second, treat the deal as at-risk and the contact as a coach, not a champion.
- **Which selling method?** Buyer knows their need → SPIN/consultative. Buyer doesn't / complex consensus B2B → Challenger (teach a reframe, arm the Mobilizer). Price is the wall → value selling (build the ROI model). Pressure/happy-ears risk → Sandler (up-front contract, surface the no). Always discover before you demo.
- **Price how?** Value-based; pick a value metric that scales with customer value (test: 2× value ≈ 2× price); package good/better/best with a premium anchor; research WTP (van Westendorp range, Gabor-Granger for the number, conjoint for tiers). Never cost-plus, never blind competitor-copy.
- **Seat or usage pricing?** Predictable budgeting + headcount-bound value → seat. Value scales with consumption + you want automatic expansion → usage (or hybrid platform-fee + usage). Whichever makes 2× value ≈ 2× price.
- **Discount?** Avoid. Trade every concession for term length, prepayment, references, or expansion. A discount for nothing trains buyers to wait and erodes the list price for everyone. (Negotiation technique → **Skill 08**.)
- **Free trial or freemium?** Fast, obvious value + want urgency/qualification → time-boxed trial. Need top-funnel volume *and* the free tier itself drives a loop (virality/data/network) → freemium. Unsure → reverse trial (full → free at expiry). Default to trial.
- **Which channel?** Bullseye: test a few, find the one that matches how the product is discovered and bought, saturate it, diversify only when it caps. Prefer compounding loops (content/viral) over linear taps (outbound/paid) for durable CAC.
- **Funnel vs loop spend?** A campaign that stops when spend stops is a tap; reinvest toward a step where output becomes next-cycle input (referral, content, expansion). Build the loop, then feed it. Optimize cycle time AND conversion-per-turn.
- **Where to invest growth effort?** Write the growth model; find the input with the biggest leak × biggest volume × biggest downstream value — and bias toward **activation/retention** over acquisition, because they compound across the whole engine.
- **Position or category-design?** Position *within* an existing category by default (Dunford's five steps). Design a new category only when no existing frame flatters you AND you can fund conditioning the market — most should not.
- **Raise price?** Yes for new customers and at renewal, after communicating value; grandfather loyal customers or phase it; never surprise-hike your base.
- **Is this account at risk?** Read leading health indicators (usage decline, champion departure, no exec sponsor, support spikes) before the renewal date; intervene early. Renewal is earned in onboarding (§6.5).

## 8. ANTI-PATTERNS (smell → fix)

- **Leaky-funnel scaling** — buying more traffic onto a low-conversion funnel. *Fix:* fix activation/retention first, then scale into the tighter funnel.
- **Leaky-bucket growth before retention** — chasing acquisition while the base churns; "growth" that's really fast bailing. *Fix:* retention-is-the-foundation — flatten the cohort curve first; NRR/GRR before logos.
- **Scaling before PMF** — pouring acquisition into a retention curve that goes to zero. *Fix:* hold spend; fix the product/aha until the curve flattens (Sean Ellis >40% + plateau + organic pull).
- **Premature scaling of a broken funnel** — hiring reps / raising ad budgets / opening regions before the unit economics and conversion rates hold. *Fix:* prove the motion's math (LTV:CAC, payback, win rate) at small scale; scale the *proven* machine, never the hope.
- **Feature-selling (not value-based)** — pitching specs not outcomes; selling the drill, not the hole. *Fix:* sell the changed business result; build the ROI/value model (§3.2); use features as evidence, not the lead.
- **Demo-before-discovery** — pitching before you know the job/pain. *Fix:* SPIN discovery first; let the buyer's implication earn the demo.
- **Single-threaded deals** — one contact, who leaves/reorgs/ghosts. *Fix:* multi-thread champion + economic buyer + users; map the buying committee.
- **Mistaking a coach for a champion** — a friendly contact who won't spend political capital. *Fix:* test the relationship (get me to the EB?); if they won't, treat the deal as unqualified.
- **Arming the Talker, not the Mobilizer** — courting the easy, agreeable contact instead of the skeptic who can drive change. *Fix:* find and equip the Mobilizer (Challenger Customer).
- **Happy ears / slow-no pipeline** — keeping unqualified deals alive because the conversation was nice; a forecast built on optimism. *Fix:* disqualify on missing EB/compelling-event/budget; Sandler up-front contract; chase a fast no.
- **No compelling event** — a deal with no dated reason to act now. *Fix:* find/build legitimate urgency from the buyer's own calendar (renewal, deadline, mandate).
- **Motion-model mismatch** — high-touch sales on a $20 product, or a signup form for a $200k committee deal. *Fix:* match the motion to the ACV ladder; PLG below the human-cost floor, field above it.
- **Cost-plus / competitor-copy pricing** — leaving value (and money) on the table or importing rivals' errors. *Fix:* value-based, tiered, WTP-researched.
- **Wrong value metric** — charging on an axis that doesn't track customer value (caps upside or punishes power users). *Fix:* re-pick the metric so 2× value ≈ 2× price; that becomes the expansion engine.
- **Reflexive discounting (discounting-as-strategy)** — eroding margin and trust, training buyers to wait, making the list price a fiction. *Fix:* trade every concession for term/prepay/references/expansion; hold the price or get something for moving it.
- **Funnel-thinking when a loop is possible** — campaigns that die when spend dies. *Fix:* engineer a reinvestment loop (referral/content/expansion), optimize cycle time and conversion-per-turn.
- **Channel spray** — thin spend across ten channels, mastery of none. *Fix:* Bullseye — find and saturate the one that fits, then diversify.
- **Optimizing blended CAC** — free organic hides loss-making paid channels. *Fix:* track paid CAC and per-channel retention separately.
- **Vanity metrics** — signups, impressions, registered users, page views, raw MRR-without-churn, downloads. *Fix:* activation, cohort retention, NRR/GRR, LTV:CAC, CAC payback, magic number, sales velocity — metrics tied to value and money.
- **Counting MRR while NRR rots** — celebrating new logos while the base leaks. *Fix:* fix retention/expansion first; NRR > 100% (and a healthy GRR floor) before you brag about growth.
- **NRR hiding a leaky GRR** — a few whales expanding masks many quietly churning. *Fix:* read GRR and NRR together; fix the floor before celebrating the net.
- **LTV on revenue not margin** — inflated LTV justifying overspend. *Fix:* LTV on gross margin; recompute LTV:CAC.
- **Tracking output, not input** — staring at the revenue number you can't directly move. *Fix:* build the growth model; manage the input metrics (signup/activation/retention/expansion); the output follows.
- **Confusing the chasm** — winning early adopters then assuming the majority follows. *Fix:* pick a beachhead segment, dominate it with references, then expand adjacently.
- **Weak/absent positioning** — a good product framed in the wrong category, losing to a worse one framed right. *Fix:* run Dunford's five-step positioning; pick the frame where your strengths are the buying criteria.
- **Category-designing to dodge the work** — inventing a category to avoid being clearly better in an existing one. *Fix:* position within first; only design a category when no frame flatters you and you can fund the conditioning.
- **CS as a cost center / reactive support** — treating success as ticket-handling, intervening only at cancellation. *Fix:* CS as a revenue function; health scores + leading indicators; QBRs that prove ROI; renewal earned in onboarding.
- **Onboarding as a feature tour** — a long upfront tutorial before any value; a blank empty state. *Fix:* shortest path to the aha; "do then explain"; seed the empty state; minimize TTV.

**HY (հակ-օրինաչափություններ՝ հոտ → ուղղում)**՝ Leaky-funnel/leaky-bucket scaling → ուղղի՛ր retention-ը առաջինը (cohort-curve հարթիր, NRR/GRR > logo)։ Scale-before-PMF / broken-funnel-ի premature scaling → ապացուցիր մաթեմը փոքր scale-ով, scale արա *ապացուցված* մեքենան։ Feature-selling → վաճառիր outcome, կառուցիր ROI-model։ Demo-before-discovery → SPIN առաջինը։ Single-thread / coach-as-champion / Talker-not-Mobilizer → multi-thread, test արա, զինի՛ր Mobilizer-ին։ Happy-ears/slow-no → disqualify, Sandler up-front contract։ Motion-model mismatch → ACV-ladder-ով համապատասխանեցրու։ Cost-plus/wrong-value-metric/discount-as-strategy → value-based, 2×value≈2×price, trade-ով discount։ Funnel-when-loop-possible → loop կառուցիր։ Channel-spray → Bullseye։ Blended-CAC/vanity-metrics/MRR-while-NRR-rots/NRR-hiding-GRR/LTV-on-revenue/output-not-input → ճիշտ metric-ները (paid-CAC, activation/retention, NRR+GRR, LTV-on-margin, input-metrics)։ Confused-chasm → beachhead։ Weak-positioning/category-dodge → Dunford-ի 5 քայլ, category-design հազվադեպ։ CS-as-cost-center → revenue function, health-score, QBR։ Onboarding-as-tour → ամենակարճ ճանապարհ դեպի aha, seed empty-state։
